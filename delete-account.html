RetainIQ — Delete Your Account

Deleting your RetainIQ account permanently and irreversibly removes your account, your uploaded documents, and all quizzes, answers and retention history from our systems. Backups purge within 30 days.

Delete from within the app

Open RetainIQ, go to Settings → Delete my account, and confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately.

Delete by email

If you can no longer access the app, email privacy@versita.co.uk from the address your account is registered under, with the subject "RetainIQ account deletion request". We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days, confirming by reply.

What is deleted

Everything: account and login, documents, generated sections and briefings, quizzes and answers, retention scores and review schedules, and subscription linkage. Subscription payments themselves are managed by Apple or Google — cancel any active subscription in your App Store or Google Play settings, as deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription.
