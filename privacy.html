Last updated: 6 September 2026 · Effective: 6 September 2026

Who we are

RetainIQ is operated by VERSITA LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales (registered office: 118 Roman Road, Winklebury, Basingstoke RG23 8HF, United Kingdom). VERSITA LIMITED is the data controller for personal data processed through the RetainIQ app. Contact: privacy@versita.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Account data — your email address and authentication credentials, to create and secure your account (legal basis: contract).

Your documents — files you upload are processed to extract text, detect sections, and generate quizzes, summaries and briefing cards. Your documents are yours: we process them solely to provide the service and never use them to train AI models, never share them with other users, and never sell them (legal basis: contract).

Learning data — quiz answers, confidence ratings and retention scores, to schedule your reviews and show your progress (legal basis: contract).

Usage and diagnostics — crash reports and product analytics (feature usage, performance), to keep the app reliable and improve it (legal basis: legitimate interests). Analytics are not used for advertising. RetainIQ contains no third-party advertising.

Payment data — subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play; we receive subscription status but never your card details.

AI processing

Document text is sent to Anthropic PBC (Claude models) to generate sections, questions and summaries. Anthropic processes this data as our processor under its commercial terms and does not train models on it.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication) in London, United Kingdom, and processed by our API service hosted on Railway (EU region). Transfers outside the UK/EEA (for example to Anthropic in the United States) are protected by appropriate safeguards, including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Your data is retained while your account is active. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account in the app, or as described on our account deletion page) permanently and irreversibly deletes your documents, quizzes, answers and retention history from our systems. Backups purge within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may access, correct, export, restrict, object to processing of, or delete your personal data. Contact privacy@versita.co.uk. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

RetainIQ is a professional tool, not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

Changes

We will notify you of material changes in-app or by email. This policy may be updated during our beta programme as the service evolves.
